Planning a peaceful getaway in Coorg? Find answers to common questions about your stay, rooms, facilities, and booking.

Still have a question?

Check-in is at 12:00 PM, and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Please contact the host in advance if you require any special arrangements.

You can enquire through our contact form or WhatsApp at 8296899910. Please share your preferred dates and guest count. Your booking is confirmed once the host verifies availability.

Our room tariff starts at ₹4,000 per night, with an additional charge of ₹750 per extra person. Please confirm the final price and availability with the host before booking.

The property has 6 rooms, including Deluxe Family Rooms with four-sharing arrangements. The standard bed capacity is 24, with a maximum accommodation of up to 30 guests, subject to the confirmed room arrangement.

Enjoy free Wi-Fi, Android TV, a hot-water kettle, fans, and food facilities. The property also offers a swimming pool, raindance, campfire, music, badminton, carrom, darts, and cycling, subject to availability and applicable arrangements.

Food is available at the property. Please contact the host to confirm the menu, meal timings, and any additional charges before your arrival.

Explore nearby attractions such as Talakaveri, Bhagamandala,Raja's Seat, Madikeri Fort, Omkareshwara Temple, Raja's Tomb,Madikeri Museum, and Mandalpatti View Point.
